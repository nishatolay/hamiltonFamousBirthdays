Thomas Jefferson

April 13th, 1743 • Shadwell, VA

Date of Death:July 4th, 1826 (83 years old), Monticello, VA Occupation: 1st Secretary Of State, 2nd Vice President and 3rd President of the USA, Founding Father Relationships: Wife, Martha Jefferson Important Events: He got elected as president in 1800, He made the Declaration of Independence in 1776, was governor of Virginia, 1779-1781. Fun Facts: He designed his own house in Monticello, He invented a swivel chair, he was fluent in french, italian, and latin and could read spanish and greek. He did not fight in the Revolutionary War.
