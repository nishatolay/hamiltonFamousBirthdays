Aaron Burr

February 6th, 1756, Newark, NJ

Date of Death: September 14th, 1836 (80 years old), Port Richmond, New York, NY Occupation: Politician, Military Officer, Lawyer, Senator Relationships: Wife, Theodosia Burr Important Events: He ran for president in 1800, he shot Alexander Hamilton in a duel and that is what led to Alexander’s death in 1804. He fought in the Revolutionary War, he ran for senator in 1791 and he was arrested for treason charges. He was tried but acquitted. Fun Facts: When Aaron Burr ran for president he was going against Thomas Jefferon and lost, when running for senator he ran against Alexander Hamilton's father in-law and that is one of the reasons for their rivalry.
