John Jay

December 12, 1745 • New York, NY

Date of Death: May 17, 1829 (83 years old), New York, NY Occupation: Governor of New York, Founding Father Relationships: Wife, Sarah Jay Important Events: He helped secure the peace treaty that ended the Revolutionary War (1783). He was also the first Chief of Justice of the United States. Fun Facts: Wrote 5 of the Federalist Papers, attended Columbia University at the age of 14, and he famously refused a British offer of independence.
