James Maddison

Birthday: March 16, 1751, Belle Grove

Date of Death: June 28, 1836 (85 years old), James Madison’s Montpelier, Montpelier Station, VA Occupation: Author, Lawyer, Farmer, Politician, 4th president of the U.S. Relationships: Wife, Dolley Madison Important Events: Member of the Continental Congress, played a key role in the Louisiana Purchase negotiations with France, led the nation through the War of 1812 against Great Britain, and wrote the Federalist Papers with Alexander Hamilton and John Jay to advocate for the Constitution's ratification. Fun Facts: There was a 17 year old age gap between Madison and his wife, Madison was the last surviving signer of the original Constitution, and Madison was the first president to wear pants instead of the traditional knee breeches. .
