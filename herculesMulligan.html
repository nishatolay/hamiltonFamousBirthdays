Hercules Mulligan

Birthday: September 25, 1740, Coleraine, United Kingdom

Date of Death: March 4, 1825 (84 years old), New York, NY Occupation: Irish-American tailor and spy Relationships: Wife, Elizabeth Sanders Important Events: He was in the Battle of Golden Hill, he was a spy for Geroge Washington in the Revolutionary War, and he helped capture British cannons. Fun Facts: He tore down the King George III statue that was in Bowling Green and melted it and used it for bullets. He immigrated to New York with his family at 6 years old. He was also buried in Trinity Church in New York City and he was buried next to Alexander Hamilton who he was great friends with.
