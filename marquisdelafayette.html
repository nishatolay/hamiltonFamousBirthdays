Marquis de Lafayette

September 6, 1757 • Chavaniac-Lafayette, France

Date of Death: May 20, 1834 (76 years old), 8th arrondissement, Paris, France Occupation: He was a politician and a military officer. Relationships: Wife, Adrienne de La Fayette Important Events: He was in the American and French Revolution. In the American Revolution, he was commissioned as a major general in the Continental Army, he was wounded while fighting in the Battle of Brandywine, and played a crucial role in the Virginia Campaign, trapping British General Cornwallis. Fun Facts: He has a very long name, he named his only son after George Washington, for he became very close with Washington and he was like a father to him, and he was known as a hero of both the American and French revolutions.
