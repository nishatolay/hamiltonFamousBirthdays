Alexander Hamilton

Birthday UNKNOWN• Charlestown, Saint Kitts and Nevis

Date of Death: July 12th, 1804, Greenwich Village, New York, NY Occupation: Author/writer, economist, lawyer, statesperson Relationships: Wife, Eliza Schulyer, Affair with Maria Reynolds Important Events: Founded the New York Post, founded the U.S. Coast Guard, helped George Washington write documents, helped fight the Revolutionary War, and published the Reynolds Pamphlet. Fun Facts: He was born in the Carribean, he was a major author of the Federalist papers, even though he was a non-presidental figure, his face is found on the ten dollar bill, and he was fluent in French.
