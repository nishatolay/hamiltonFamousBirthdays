Elizabeth Hamilton

Birthday: August 9, 1757, Albany, NY

Date of Death: November 9th, 1854 (97 years old), Washington, D.C. Occupation: Philanthropist Relationships: Husband, Alexander Hamilton Important Events: On December 14th, 1780, she got married, and her husband died in a duel in 1804. Fun Facts: She raised funds for the Washington Monument in D.C., and she cofounded NYC’s first orphanage. She grew up and was friends with Martha Washington who was Geroge Washington’s wife. .
