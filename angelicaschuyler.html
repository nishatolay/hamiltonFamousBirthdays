Angelica Schulyer

Birthday: February 20, 1756, Albany, NY

Date of Death: March 13, 1814 (58 years old), Manhattan, NY Occupation: Socialite Relationships: Husband, John Barker Church Important Events: Lived in Europe for 16 years, she befriended figures like Benjamin Franklin, Thomas Jefferson, and Marquis de Lafayette. She became a part of high society in London, even meeting royalty. Fun Facts: There’s a town named after her, she supported her sister Eliza when her husband was found cheating, and she had four siblings.
